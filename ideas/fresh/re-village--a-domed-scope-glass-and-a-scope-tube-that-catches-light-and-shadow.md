# A domed scope glass, and a scope tube that catches light and shadow

Order: 4006
From: the ideas repo, `games/re-village.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/re-village.md>), copied 2026-10-01

`[raw]` · `[not judged]` — *not checked against the game*

> "make the scope glass domed and have the picture be domed as well - like an old monitor not a
> flat screen. have the high magnification scope inside tube part react to light and cast shadows
> over the inside of the tube and the scope picture glass" — 2026-09-29

Two looks, both about making the scope feel like a real object rather than a flat picture:
1. **Domed glass** — the lens bulges slightly, and the picture on it bends with the bulge, like an
   old curved TV screen.
2. **A lit tube** — the inside of the scope tube reacts to the light around it, so shadows fall
   across the tube walls and across the picture itself.

**What it'd take:** (1) the mod already draws the picture onto the glass in its own step, so a
bulge is likely a matter of bending that picture as it is drawn. (2) is harder: it needs the
game's lighting to reach our scope, or a faked shadow that follows the light direction.
⚠️ **Unchecked** — and a sibling of the glass-shine idea above; both would be judged in the headset.
