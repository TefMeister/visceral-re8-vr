# The right grip holds the gun, the aim is gone, and accuracy comes from how you hold it

Order: 4011

**What Tefa said (2026-10-25):** the right grip must be held to keep the gun in the hand; let go and it goes back to its
holster. That means no aim stance at all. Later: accuracy set by movement and by the two-handed grip.

**Built 2026-10-25 (unworn):** letting go of the right grip puts the gun away (Andyalpa's own stow); the grip no longer
presses the game's aim. The knife is left as it was.

**What it'd take (the accuracy half):** the game's spread or sway value, set each frame from how fast the head and the
gun hand move and whether the left hand holds the gun too. Same family as *Sniper spread to zero* above.

Same rule wanted in every RE game: [`resident-evil-all.md`](resident-evil-all.md) → "The right grip holds the weapon, and a stick dodge".
