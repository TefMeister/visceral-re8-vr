# Numbered pairs: a green activation sphere sends the hand to its yellow landing spot

Order: 4013
From: the ideas repo, `games/re-village.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/re-village.md>), copied 2026-10-25

`[raw]` · `[looks doable]` for the pairs and the hand travel, `[hard]` for the colours and numbers in the game — *checked against the code on 2026-10-25 (zones, the rack's take, the ease), the colours not tried*

**What Tefa said (2026-10-25):** green spheres are activation areas, yellow spots are where the hand lands once it is
activated in its sphere; with several, they are numbered, and green 3 sends the hand to yellow 3.

**What it'd take:** (1) the pairs: each activation zone gets a number and a landing spot of the same number, both placed
and sized in the Grab Zones app; the left grip pressed in zone N eases the hand (the arc ease the rack uses) onto spot N.
The rack's "take at the grip zone, land on the bolt" (built 2026-10-25) is already one such pair. `MODEL: OPUS`.
(2) the look: the game only draws the spheres with the six looks tried (own, light pillar, glass, liquid, mold, axis),
no free colour; green and yellow need two looks that differ, or a tint not found yet; numbers floating in the game are
harder still, so the app shows the numbers first. `MODEL: FABLE` for that part.
