# Three reload modes: immersive, semi-immersive, automatic

Order: 4015
From: the ideas repo, `games/re-village.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/re-village.md>), copied 2026-11-01

`[raw]` · `[looks doable]` — *judged from the mod's code only: every step already exists as its own piece; not tried*

**What Tefa said (2026-11-01):** once the hand reloads are finished and tuned, a choice of three: **immersive** (what is
being built now: every step by hand), **semi-immersive** (B opens and closes everything, the player only reaches for the
pouch and brings the new ammo), **automatic** (B does the whole sequence, with better animations). Immersive first, until
its mechanics are rock solid.

**What it'd take:** a setting the reload, rack and breech code reads at each step: whether a step waits for the hands or
runs by itself on B. The "better animations" part means the game's own reload animations playing with the hands on the gun
(an earlier idea on this page, left for Fable).
