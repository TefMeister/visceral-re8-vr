# A light scope for lower-end PCs: the game's own scope view, just the round glass

Order: 4010
From: the ideas repo, `games/re-village.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/re-village.md>), copied 2026-10-01

`[raw]` · `[not judged]` — *Tefa: "something for later"*

> "even a 5080 shows that it's pretty heavy rendering the scope and the game at the same time and for
> people with lower end pc's, we need to just modify the original - so when RG is pressed then the game
> goes into scope mode, the weapon model dissapears and they will have a nice clear scope picture - just
> the round glass." — 2026-09-28

A second, cheaper version of the scope mod. Instead of drawing the world twice every frame (once for
the eyes, once for the scope), it uses the game's own zoomed scope view: pressing RG switches the game
into its scope mode, the rifle model is hidden, and the player sees a clean round glass picture. One
drawing of the world instead of two, so the cost of the scope all but disappears.

Tefa ties it to a later job: finding a way to bring the scope to the flat screen as well. Both start
from the game's own scope view rather than our second drawing, so they are likely to share the work.

⚠️ **Not judged.** Nobody has checked how the game's own scope view behaves inside the VR mod, or
whether it can be shown as a round glass picture without the rest of the screen.
