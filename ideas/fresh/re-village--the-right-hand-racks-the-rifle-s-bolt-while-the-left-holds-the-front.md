# The right hand racks the rifle's bolt while the left holds the front

Order: 4014
From: the ideas repo, `games/re-village.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/re-village.md>), copied 2026-11-01

`[raw]` · `[hard]` — *judged from the mod's code only (rack.cpp, the left-hand bolt), not tried in the game*

**What Tefa said (2026-11-01):** with the rifle held by the left grip on its front part, holding LT lets RG send the
RIGHT hand onto the bolt to rack it, the way a real bolt rifle is worked. The left hand can still rack it as today.

**What it'd take:** the bolt cycle itself already exists (LT lifts the bolt, the hand pulls it back and pushes it home,
both clicks), but it is driven by the left hand. The hard part is the gun: in the game it hangs off the right hand, so
while the right hand is on the bolt the gun has to be carried by the left hand at the front instead, held still where it
was, then handed back to the right hand when RG is let go. Unknown until tried: whether the gun can be held from the
left hand without the game's own arm animation pulling it back. A Fable job. `MODEL: FABLE`.
